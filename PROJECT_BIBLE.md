# LEGACY PROJECT BIBLE

## App Name

Legacy

---

## Slogan

Build the Father. Leave the Legacy.

---

## Mission

Legacy helps fathers become more intentional through reflection, accountability, meaningful moments, teaching, and preserving memories for future generations.

Legacy does not make a better father.

The father builds himself.

Legacy is simply the tool that helps him stay intentional.

---

## Vision

To become the world's most intentional fatherhood platform.

Not by replacing fathers—

but by helping fathers become the men their children deserve.

---

## Core Principles

1. Intentionality
Every feature should encourage intentional fatherhood.

2. Growth
The father is always growing.

3. Teaching
Wisdom should be passed down intentionally.

4. Legacy
The greatest inheritance is character.

5. Accountability
The app keeps fathers accountable.
It does not replace responsibility.

---

## Official Motto

Legacy doesn't build the father.

The father builds himself.

Legacy simply helps him stay intentional.

---

## Target Audience

• New Fathers

• Experienced Fathers

• Single Fathers

• Co-Parents

• Grandfathers raising grandchildren

Anyone who wants to become more intentional with the children in their lives.

---

## Version 1 Goal

Help fathers become more intentional every single day.

Everything else is secondary.
